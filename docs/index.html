Inside the app

Eight spaces. One personal journal.

The whole day on one screen

A greeting that changes with the time of day, activity rings for calories, steps and sleep, and a weekly chart you can switch between steps, workouts and minutes. Page back through earlier weeks or jump to a date with the calendar.

My body keeps your weight trend with the lowest, highest and total change, next to measurements and progress photos. Below it sit your challenges and the day note.

  • Rings for calories, steps and sleep
  • Weekly chart with best day
  • Weight trend you can edit
  • Active challenges at a glance
  • Day note with prompts
  • “How this is calculated” on every metric

From a short walk to a full gym day

Pick one of seven activities and one of 50 subtypes, from trail running and Nordic walking to Vinyasa yoga and table tennis. Every type logs the numbers that make sense for it: sets and weight for the gym, time for yoga, distance for runs and rides, score for sport.

Outdoors, switch on route recording and follow the session live on OpenStreetMap. Finishing takes a long press, so a tap in your pocket won't end the run.

  • 50 subtypes in 7 activities
  • Sets, reps, weight, time, distance, score
  • Rest timer and interval guide
  • Templates, favorites and schedule
  • Live GPS route with pace
  • Personal bests per exercise

Leafit Wrapped: a card worth posting

Turn a saved workout or a finished challenge into a clean card. Leafit picks the headline number and adds context such as “Farthest yet”, “New personal best!” or how you did compared to last time.

Show the route on a map, as a plain outline, or hide it completely. Then save it to Pictures/Leafit or send it through the share sheet. Nothing is ever posted for you.

  • Poster, Journal and Result focus layouts
  • Mint, Sunrise and Lavender palettes
  • Card, 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, sticker PNG
  • Custom title and caption
  • Choose which numbers to show
  • Map, outline or no route

Meals and water without the spreadsheet feel

Calories against your goal and the protein, fat and carbs split for the day. Breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner each have their own group. Add a dish from your products by grams, type it in once, or reuse a saved meal.

Water is one tap: 150, 250 or 500 ml, with undo for the last glass and reminders that stay quiet from 22:00 to 08:00.

  • Daily calories and macros
  • Four meal groups
  • Own products per 100 g
  • Saved meals
  • Quick water portions
  • Recipes, meal plan, shopping list

A sleep tracker that listens, and alarms that just work

Your sleep shows last night, a 7-night average, weekly sleep debt, your usual bedtime and how regular you are. It even suggests when to go to bed to feel rested for your next alarm.

Log a night by hand or with the moon-and-sun dial, or let the tracker record it while the phone lies on the mattress.

  • Background sleep tracker
  • Night report with sound clips
  • Separate alarm list
  • Moon-and-sun dial
  • Quality rating and tags
  • Sleep debt and schedule chart

Sleep stages are not measured. The cycle chart is an illustration, and sound labels are estimates, not medical advice.

Goals that count what you already do

18 badges unlock from your own journal, from “First workout” to “Dream journal”. A ring shows how far you are, the closest badge is always on top, and filters split earned from still ahead.

32 challenge ideas in five categories, or a goal of your own with a name, metric, daily threshold and color. Up to three at once, for 7, 14 or 30 days.

  • Progress ring and filters
  • No streak shaming
  • Custom challenges
  • Counted automatically
  • Share a finished challenge
  • Hide the block if you want

Set up in a minute, then forget about it

First launch takes three short steps: name, photo and language, then age, height and weight with big − and + buttons. BMI and a daily calorie goal show up right away, and body measurements are optional. Moving from another phone? Restore a backup from the very first screen.

The profile keeps your goals for steps, water and sleep, and an activity map of the last 49 days.

  • Three-step first run
  • BMI and calorie goal
  • Neutral “Leaf” gender option
  • Photo crop with pinch to zoom
  • 49-day activity map
  • Goals you adjust with − / +

Settings you can actually find

Search any option by name. Light, dark or system theme, wallpaper colors on Android 12 and newer, and three languages you can switch without restarting your phone.

The activity section explains in plain words what runs in the background and why, and tells you when the system is restricting Leafit. Export, import and a full reset sit together under Data.

Sleep

Put the phone by your pillow. Sleep.

The tracker runs in the background all night and builds a report you can actually read in the morning.

What the tracker does

  • Adapts to your room's background noise
  • Keeps short clips of louder moments, with two seconds before each
  • Marks what sounds like snoring or talking
  • Optional short vibration when it hears snoring
  • Notes restless minutes with the accelerometer
  • Recording delay, so falling asleep isn't logged as noise
  • Optional sleep sound while you drift off

Room loudness by minute. Tap a sound to listen, or play the night's clips one after another.

Small things, done properly

It keeps working outside the app

Six home screen widgets

My day, Sleep, Workout, Water, Food and This week. Each one adapts to its size, from a single goal ring at 1×1 to a full card with a chart and a button at 4×3. The workout widget shows a live timer, sets or GPS distance while a session runs.

Live notifications

One clear layout for every session: a big timer or value, progress and up to three tiles. Pause, resume or skip rest from the shade. On Android 16 a session can sit in the status bar as a chip.

Haptics you can tune

Different short patterns for selecting, pressing, starting, pausing and confirming. Set the strength, or turn them off.

App shortcuts

Long-press the icon for + Water, Workout and Log sleep.

Maps without an API key

Routes are drawn on OpenStreetMap through MapLibre.

Three languages

English, Ukrainian and Russian, switchable in the app.

Design

Material 3 Expressive, all the way through

Leafit follows Google's newest design language: shapes that morph when you press them, connected button groups, wavy progress, filled Material Symbols and spring motion. It looks at home next to the system on Android 16, and still runs on Android 8.

Honest numbers

Every number tells you where it came from

Tap “How this is calculated” under a metric and Leafit explains it in one or two sentences. No black boxes, no made-up precision.

Calorie goal

A resting estimate from your weight, height, age and sex, multiplied by 1.2. Without a profile it falls back to 2000 kcal.

Training volume

Repetitions × weight, summed over the sets you marked as completed.

Steps

Read from your phone's step sensor. If there is no sensor, Leafit says so instead of guessing.

Sleep

Recorded time in bed from start to waking. Stages are not measured, and the cycle chart says so.

Privacy

Your journal stays on your phone

Leafit keeps everything in a local database. There is no Leafit account and no server behind the app, so there is simply nowhere for your data to go. Sleep audio is analyzed on the device and only the short clips are kept. Sharing a card is always something you do on purpose.

  • No account
  • No ads or analytics
  • Works offline
  • JSON backup

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no premium tier, no ads. Leafit is open source under GPL-3.0. If you want to say thanks, there is a Ko-fi page, but that's completely optional.

How do I install the APK?

Download Leafit.apk on your phone and open it. Android will ask you once to allow installs from your browser or file manager. After that it installs like any other app.

Why isn't it on Google Play?

Not yet. For now the signed APK is published on GitHub Releases, and every release is built from the public source code.

I have version 2.0. How do I update?

Version 2.5 is signed with a new key, so Android won't install it over 2.0. Export your data in Settings → Data, uninstall the old version, install 2.5 and import the file. This is a one-time step, future updates install normally.

Will the sleep tracker drain my battery?

It runs all night, so plug the phone in, as the app suggests. Some phones stop background apps at night; Leafit tells you how to remove the battery restriction if that happens.

Which phones does it work on?

Anything with Android 8.0 or newer. It's tested on a Samsung Galaxy S10 (Android 12), Xiaomi 15 (HyperOS, Android 15 and 16) and Pixel 7 (Android 16 and 17). GPS routes need Google Play services; step counting needs a step sensor.

Does Leafit read health data from other apps?

No. It counts steps with the phone's own sensor and everything else comes from what you log. There is no Health Connect sync at the moment.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Open an issue on GitHub with your phone model, Android version and steps to reproduce. Translations and pull requests are welcome too.
